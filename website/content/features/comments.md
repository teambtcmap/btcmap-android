# Comments

Comments let you leave a note about a place for other people to read — for
example whether a merchant still accepts bitcoin, or how the payment went.

## Reading comments

Open a place to see a preview of its comments and the number of comments. If
there are any, tapping **Comments** opens the full list. Comments are read from
your device, so the list appears instantly and works offline. Dates are shown in
your device's time zone and language.

## Adding a comment

Comments are **anonymous**, but a small fee in sats is charged as a spam
protection measure. Adding one does not require an account, but it does require
a Lightning wallet on your device.

1. Open a place and tap **Comment** (or **Add comment** if there are none yet).
2. Type your comment. It cannot be empty.
3. Continue to request the current fee.
4. Pay the invoice with a compatible wallet — either scan the QR code or use the
   **Pay** button, and copy the invoice if your wallet needs it.
5. Once the payment is confirmed, your comment is posted and a message confirms
   it.

If you do not have a compatible wallet, the app tells you so.

## Notes

- A comment is submitted to BTC Map and becomes visible to everyone once the
  payment is confirmed; it can take a moment to appear.
- If a sync fails, the comments screen reports the problem instead of claiming
  there are no comments.
- Comments are cached locally, so previously synced comments remain readable
  offline.
