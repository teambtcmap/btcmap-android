# Exchanges

Bitcoin ATMs and currency exchanges are the exception to the map's merchant
focus: they are hidden from the default view, so switch to the **Exchanges**
filter to see them.

## ATMs and exchanges

- **Bitcoin ATMs** let you buy bitcoin with cash, and some also sell it.
- **Currency exchanges** are counters where you can buy or sell bitcoin in
  person, usually alongside regular currency exchange.

Tapping a marker opens the same card as a merchant, with its address, opening
hours, website and actions. See [Merchants](merchants.md) for what the card
shows and how to report a problem.

![The map with the Exchanges filter selected, showing bitcoin ATM and currency exchange markers in Geneva](/images/exchanges-map-light.png)
![The card for a currency exchange, showing its address, phone, last verified date and actions](/images/exchange-card-light.png)

ATMs and exchanges come from OpenStreetMap, like merchants. If one is missing or
out of date, see [Merchants](merchants.md) for how edits work.
